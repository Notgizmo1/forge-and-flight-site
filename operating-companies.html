Operating Companies

Vertically Integrated Capabilities

Four specialized operating companies delivering mission-ready solutions across the complete unmanned systems lifecycle—from component manufacturing through operational deployment.

Complete Lifecycle Support

Each operating company maintains specialized expertise in its domain while leveraging shared infrastructure, intellectual property, and quality frameworks. This vertical integration eliminates external dependencies and ensures complete NDAA Section 848 compliance across the supply chain.

Forge and Flight Labs, LLC

Aircraft Design & Manufacturing

North Carolina-based advanced fabrication and autonomous aircraft development studio dedicated to pushing the boundaries of modern aerospace engineering. Specializes in Group 2/3 unmanned aircraft systems combining VTOL capability with extended endurance and advanced autonomy.

Visit Website

Core Capabilities

  • End-to-end aircraft development from concept through field deployment
  • Advanced additive manufacturing and composite fabrication
  • Autonomous systems integration (ArduPilot, NVIDIA Jetson, BotBlox)
  • VTOL and fixed-wing platform design
  • Custom sensor and payload integration
  • Flight testing with FAA-certified pilot oversight

Platform Portfolio

  • AETHER Series: Electric-powered endurance platforms (2-5 hours)
  • VANGUARD Series: Hybrid VTOL platforms (6-12 hours)
  • TITAN Series: Heavy-lift gas-powered platforms
