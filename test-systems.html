Flight Test Division

Developmental flight testing, performance validation, and customer acceptance demonstrations for unmanned aircraft systems. Dedicated test range infrastructure proximate to Fort Liberty, Fayetteville, NC.

Test Services

  • Developmental flight testing and envelope expansion
  • Performance validation and characterization
  • Mission profile demonstration and simulation
  • Customer acceptance testing and delivery verification
  • Operator training and platform familiarization flights
  • Regulatory compliance verification for FAA operations

Infrastructure

  • Dedicated test range proximate to Fort Liberty, NC
  • Professional Range Safety Officer oversight
  • FAA airspace coordination and LAANC management
  • Instrumented range for data capture and analysis
  • Coordinated controlled airspace for BVLOS testing

Division Status

The Flight Test Division currently operates as an internal division of Forge & Flight Holdings, Inc., supporting platform production for Forge and Flight Labs. Formal standalone operations and external customer services are planned for 2026 as capital permits.

For NDAA compliance documentation on platforms produced by Forge and Flight Labs, contact us directly.
