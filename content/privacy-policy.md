Forge & Flight Holdings, Inc. ("Holdings," "we," "us") operates forgeandflight.com and its associated subpages. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form, including your name, organization, email address, phone number, and the content of your message. We do not collect payment information through this website.

We also collect standard web analytics data through Google Analytics, including page views, session duration, device type, and approximate geographic location (derived from IP address). This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

## How We Use Information

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information with our subsidiaries (Forge & Flight Labs LLC, Forge and Flight Academy LLC) to the extent necessary to respond to your inquiry.

## Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for business correspondence purposes. You may request deletion of your submitted information by contacting us at m.ortiz@forgeandflight.com.

## Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Analytics for traffic measurement and Cloudflare for content delivery and security. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not use advertising networks or tracking pixels.
