Why SAT-Zone Contracting

Fast acquisition. Small business priority. No competitive overhead.

Every federal acquisition at or below $350,000 moves through FAR Part 13 simplified acquisition procedures — which means dramatically reduced procurement timelines, mandatory small business set-aside (the Rule of Two), and no formal competitive range requirements. For contracting officers, it means faster award. For Forge & Flight, it means first-contract opportunities that build the past performance record to compete on larger awards.

Fort Bragg's contracting offices — MICC-Fort Bragg (419th CSB), USASOC HQ Contracting, and the Fort Bragg Small Business Office — award tens of millions annually in this range across our four capability areas. Our physical proximity, active SAM registration, and CMMC Level 2 compliance position us to respond and execute rapidly.

Defense & Security Consulting

Twenty years of operational experience. Available as a consulting service.

Services are available to defense contractors, government agencies, think tanks, and private sector clients.

Defense & National Security Consulting

Threat assessments, operational planning support, red team advisory, and program support for mid-tier defense primes, DoD program offices, and private security firms. Rate range: $125–$275/hour. Retainer structures available.

Managed Intelligence Services

Open-source intelligence analysis, geopolitical risk assessment, due diligence research, and threat actor profiling for corporate security teams, law firms, and private equity firms. Products delivered as structured written assessments with sourced analysis. Contact for pricing and service tiers.
