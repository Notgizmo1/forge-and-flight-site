The Company

Defense technology built from the inside out.

Forge & Flight Holdings was founded in January 2026 by two career military professionals with combined decades of operational experience spanning Special Operations, aviation, aeronautical engineering, and defense acquisition.

The company exists because the gap between what operators need and what the defense industrial base delivers has widened to a point where the mission suffers. We build what we would have wanted in the field — NDAA-compliant, domestically sourced, CMMC-hardened platforms and software that integrate cleanly into existing DoD infrastructure without requiring a systems integrator between the product and the operator.

Incorporated in North Carolina and headquartered in Fayetteville, we operate within the Fort Bragg ecosystem intentionally. Proximity to the 18th Airborne Corps, USASOC, Special Operations Command, and the MICC-Fort Bragg contracting office is not incidental — it is strategy.

Strategic Location

Fayetteville, NC.
Fort Bragg next door.

Forge & Flight is headquartered in Fayetteville, NC — home to Fort Bragg, the 82nd Airborne Division, the 18th Airborne Corps, United States Army Special Operations Command (USASOC), and Pope Field. It is the largest military installation in the world by population.

That proximity is not coincidence. The Mission and Installation Contracting Command (MICC-Fort Bragg) awards approximately $1 billion annually in contracts. USASOC hosts monthly small business capability briefings. The North Carolina Military Business Center at Fayetteville Technical Community College has helped NC businesses win over $18.6 billion in federal contracts.

We are not a defense company trying to reach the DoD from the outside. We operate within the ecosystem, understand the culture, and speak the language — literally and operationally.
