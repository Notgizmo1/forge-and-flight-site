Why Domestic Matters

American-made. Fully documented. Field-ready.

Every Forge & Flight platform is designed from the ground up for NDAA compliance — not retrofit. That means complete component-level traceability documentation, no prohibited foreign electronics in any data or power path, and supply chains built to sustain government audit requirements over the full program life.

NDAA Section 848 Compliance

All platforms are compliant with NDAA Section 848 prohibitions on covered unmanned aircraft systems. Full BOM-level documentation available for procurement officers and program offices requiring written compliance certification.

Blue UAS Framework Eligibility

The Aether-10 platform is aligned with the Defense Innovation Unit Blue UAS program framework. Procurement officers can reference Blue UAS documentation as part of their NDAA compliance record. Contact for current certification status.

Open Architecture Design

All platforms use open flight controller standards — ArduPilot and PX4 compatible — with standard MAVLink interfaces. No proprietary ground station lock-in. Integrates with ATAK, existing DoD C2 infrastructure, and the AegisEye Intelligence Suite modules.

Field Maintainability

Designed for organic maintenance by trained operators. Modular airframe construction, standardized fasteners, and replaceable components available through NDAA-compliant domestic supply chains. Forge Flex retainer programs provide ongoing parts access and technical support.
