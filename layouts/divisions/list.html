Subsidiary LLC · UAS Training & Certification

Forge and Flight Academy LLC

CAGE 1A6J3 · UEI YV8UNYJWZHV1 · SAM Active through Mar 9, 2027 · Fayetteville, NC

Forge and Flight Academy is the professional UAS training subsidiary of Holdings. It delivers a 10-course catalog spanning FAA Part 107 certification, FPV tactical operations, advanced fabrication, AI-enabled drone systems, and instructor certification programs. The Academy serves DoD units, federal agencies, universities, and defense contractors — with a Five-Pillar Framework designed to build operators from ground up through mission-ready proficiency.

Electronics Manufacturing Division

Operating under Holdings CAGE 18WR3 · UEI Z9PWMMZXJ321 · Fayetteville, NC
