What Forge Flex Provides

One contract. All of the capability.

Forge Flex eliminates the procurement friction that delays UAS development and experimentation programs. Rather than navigating separate vendors, contracts, and timelines for components, manufacturing, and engineering support — one retainer covers everything.

Procurement Services

NDAA-compliant component sourcing and vendor management
Flight controllers, compute modules, propulsion, GPS/RTK systems
Receiving, inspection, and inventory management
Full documentation trail for NDAA audit compliance
Standard components delivered in 3–5 business days

Manufacturing Services

3D printing — FDM, SLA, and DLP

Composite layup and CNC machining coordination
Complete aircraft builds from specification to flight-ready
Airframe fabrication — carbon fiber, foam core, custom kits

Engineering Services

Dedicated program manager at enterprise tiers
Work order coordination and progress reporting
Monthly statements — labor hours and procurement activity
Audit-ready documentation for all procurements

Service Tiers — Bronze through Obsidian

Six retainer tiers from entry-level pilot programs to enterprise-scale partnerships. Tiers include allocated labor hours and a procurement ceiling. Unused procurement balance carries over at end of year (20–30% depending on tier). Labor hours do not carry over.

Enterprise tiers (Platinum and above) include a dedicated program manager, priority queue status, and a 24-hour response guarantee. Diamond and Obsidian tiers support embedded team options and dedicated inventory storage.

End-of-fiscal-year fund absorption: Forge Flex retainers can be structured to obligate O&M and RDT&E funds immediately with work performed over the contract period. Contracts executable within two weeks of award.
