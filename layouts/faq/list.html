NDAA Compliance

Are your platforms NDAA Section 848 compliant?

Yes. All Forge & Flight Labs platforms — Longbow-8, Aether-10, Vanguard-14, and Titan-20 — are NDAA Section 848 compliant. Written compliance documentation is provided at the component level for every procurement. No Chinese-manufactured components appear in any data path, power path, or communication system.

Are your platforms on the Blue UAS Cleared List?

The Aether-10 is aligned with the Defense Innovation Unit Blue UAS program framework. Contact us for current certification status. Procurement officers who need compliance documentation independent of Blue UAS listing can request our component-level BOM compliance package.

What does NDAA §889 compliance mean for your software?

All Forge & Flight software products — Forge Command Suite and AegisEye Intelligence Suite — contain no Huawei, ZTE, Hytera, Hikvision, or Dahua components in any code path or network stack. Compliance is verified through CI pipeline and annual supply chain audit.

CMMC & Cybersecurity

What is your CMMC Level 2 status?

Forge & Flight Holdings has completed a CMMC Level 2 self-assessment with a perfect SPRS score of 110/110 — all 110 NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 controls implemented. Score has been uploaded to sprs.csd.disa.mil. Holdings is fully eligible for DoD contracts requiring CMMC Level 2. C3PAO third-party assessment is planned for late 2026.

Can your software be deployed in a CUI environment?

The Forge Command Suite is hardened to CMMC Level 2 standards and designed for environments handling Controlled Unclassified Information. All network traffic is end-to-end encrypted. AegisEye GCS supports PIV/CAC smart card authentication. Contact us for detailed technical security documentation.

Platform & Software Technical

Do your AI detection modules require cloud connectivity?

No. All AegisEye Intelligence Suite modules run entirely on edge hardware on the platform. Inference is local. Alerts deliver via MQTT and ATAK CoT. All modules are specifically designed for disconnected and EMCON operation — no cloud required for any detection or alert function.

What ATAK integration is available?

IronMesh provides bidirectional ATAK/TAK Server integration — drone position, telemetry, video, ADS-B, and mission commands via standard CoT. AegisEye Intelligence Suite delivers georeferenced detection alerts directly to ATAK with color-coded severity. GoTo commands, mission uploads, and geofence operations are executable from ATAK.
