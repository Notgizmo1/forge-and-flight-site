Why Forge & Flight Holdings

What separates a holding company from a vendor is accountability. Every capability in our ecosystem — design, electronics, testing, training — operates under unified ownership with a single point of contact, a single CAGE code, and a single mission.

Complete NDAA Compliance

Section 848 compliance with verifiable domestic supply chains, component-level traceability records, and compliance certificates provided at delivery. No waivers required. Audit-ready documentation for government acquisition files.

Vertically Integrated

Platform design, electronics manufacturing, flight test, and operator training under one corporate roof. No handoffs to foreign vendors, no integration gaps, no competing interests between suppliers.

Dedicated Test Infrastructure

Our Flight Test Division operates a dedicated test range proximate to Fort Bragg — eliminating development bottlenecks, accelerating acceptance testing, and enabling rapid iteration cycles without third-party scheduling dependencies.

Open Architecture

ArduPilot-based flight control, NVIDIA edge compute, and documented API access prevent vendor lock-in and enable customer modification, long-term platform sustainability, and integration with existing systems.

Strategic Location

Fayetteville, NC — proximate to Fort Bragg (82nd Airborne, SOCOM), the Research Triangle's university ecosystem, and the North Carolina aerospace supply chain corridor. Rapid response to operational customer requirements.
