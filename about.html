Corporate Overview

Forge & Flight Holdings, Inc. is a North Carolina-based aerospace holding company established in January 2026 to address critical gaps in American unmanned systems capabilities. As federal agencies phase out foreign-manufactured platforms, the need for vertically integrated domestic suppliers has become urgent.

We provide strategic management and governance across four specialized operating companies, enabling rapid development and deployment of NDAA-compliant unmanned aircraft systems from component-level through operational deployment.

Mission & Core Values

Mission First

Every design decision, manufacturing process, and business practice prioritizes operational effectiveness for warfighters and researchers. We exist to solve mission-critical problems.

Domestic Security

Commitment to American manufacturing, American jobs, and elimination of foreign supply chain dependencies in critical defense systems. Complete NDAA Section 848 compliance across all operations.

Partnership

Collaborative relationships with customers, treating them as mission partners rather than transactions, ensuring platforms evolve with operational needs.

Leadership & Governance

Forge & Flight Holdings is led by experienced aerospace executives with backgrounds in unmanned systems development, DoD program management, defense electronics manufacturing, and government contracting. Our leadership team brings decades of combined experience in aerospace engineering, flight test operations, and NDAA compliance frameworks.

Executive Leadership

Our executive team combines deep technical expertise with operational experience across defense contracting, unmanned systems development, and aerospace manufacturing. Leadership backgrounds include:

  • DoD program management and systems engineering
  • Unmanned aircraft design and flight test operations
  • Defense electronics and avionics development
  • Government contracting and NDAA compliance
  • Manufacturing operations and supply chain management

Board of Directors

Our Board provides strategic oversight ensuring operational excellence and mission alignment across all operating companies. Board composition includes industry veterans with experience in:

  • Aerospace engineering and platform development
  • DoD acquisition and procurement processes
  • Corporate governance and fiduciary responsibility
  • Quality management and compliance frameworks
  • Capital allocation and strategic planning

Vertical Integration Strategy

Our vertical integration approach eliminates the fragmentation that has constrained American UAS development. By maintaining control from electronics manufacturing through operator training, we deliver complete solutions while ensuring supply chain security and rapid development cycles.
