Corporate Overview

Forge & Flight Holdings, Inc. is a North Carolina-based aerospace holding company established in January 2026 to address critical gaps in American unmanned systems capabilities. As federal agencies phase out foreign-manufactured platforms, the need for vertically integrated domestic suppliers has become urgent.

We provide strategic management and governance across four specialized operating companies, enabling rapid development and deployment of NDAA-compliant unmanned aircraft systems from component-level through operational deployment.

Mike is a retired U.S. Army Special Operations veteran with 20 years of service spanning 10 deployments across tactical and strategic environments. His operational background includes embassy postings in South America and direct engagement with U.S. Ambassadors on regional security policy and counterterrorism operations.

A graduate of Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University with a B.S. in Aeronautical Science and a minor in Space Physics, Mike founded Forge & Flight Holdings to address a gap he identified directly from operational experience: the United States lacked a vertically integrated, NDAA-compliant unmanned systems company capable of delivering platforms, training, electronics, and test infrastructure under a single domestic roof.

His technical background spans UAS platform design and fabrication, RF systems and spectrum management, autonomy and AI systems integration, and Python development and automation. He brings the same precision-execution standard to business operations that Special Operations demands in the field.

Operating Philosophy

Every decision at Forge & Flight Holdings is evaluated against three criteria: does it strengthen supply chain security, does it reduce operator dependency on foreign components, and does it advance American capability in unmanned systems.

  • Precision execution over speed
  • Long-term leverage and control
  • Cost efficiency without capability compromise
  • Independence from external dependencies

Vertical Integration Strategy

Our vertical integration approach eliminates the fragmentation that has constrained American UAS development. By maintaining control from electronics manufacturing through operator training, we deliver complete solutions while ensuring supply chain security and rapid development cycles.
