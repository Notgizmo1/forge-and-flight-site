Why Vertical Integration Matters

The displacement of Chinese-manufactured drones has exposed critical vulnerabilities: fragmented supply chains, foreign component dependencies, and lack of integrated support. Our vertically integrated approach addresses these challenges directly.

  • Supply Chain Security: Domestic electronics manufacturing ensures NDAA compliance and Blue UAS Framework eligibility without external dependencies or foreign component risks.
  • Rapid Development: Integrated test infrastructure eliminates facility access bottlenecks that extend competitor development timelines by 12-24 months.
  • Complete Solutions: Platform delivery includes operator training and sustainment support—ensuring mission readiness, not just hardware delivery.
  • Cost Efficiency: Internal component manufacturing and shared infrastructure enable competitive pricing for state, local, and university customers.
