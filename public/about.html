About Forge & Flight Holdings

Strategic management and integration across American aerospace operations

Who We Are

Forge & Flight Holdings, Inc. is a U.S.-based aerospace holding company formed to provide strategic management, governance, and integration across specialized aerospace operating businesses.

The company was created to address fragmentation commonly seen across aerospace design, manufacturing, training, electronics, and flight operations. By centralizing leadership and standards at the corporate level, Forge & Flight Holdings enables its operating companies to execute efficiently while maintaining unified strategic direction.

Operating Structure

Forge & Flight Holdings does not directly manufacture products or deliver training services. Instead, the company provides strategic oversight and enabling functions that allow operating companies to focus on execution excellence.

Intellectual Property

Centralized ownership and licensing of proprietary designs, processes, and technical documentation protecting innovations while enabling rapid technology transfer across operating companies.

Quality & Compliance

Enterprise-level quality frameworks and compliance oversight ensuring consistent standards while enabling operating companies to obtain certifications based on customer requirements.

Financial Governance

Capital allocation, financial controls, and strategic investment decisions supporting sustainable growth and disciplined resource deployment across the portfolio.

Strategic Integration

Cross-subsidiary technical coordination and program alignment enabling integrated solutions that leverage capabilities across multiple operating companies.

Why Vertical Integration

The American UAS industry faces critical challenges that demand integrated solutions:

  • Foreign Component Dependencies: Chinese electronics manufacturers dominate global supply chains, creating NDAA compliance barriers for defense and government customers requiring verifiable domestic sourcing.
  • Fragmented Infrastructure: Lack of dedicated test facilities forces manufacturers into shared-use arrangements, extending development timelines and limiting iteration cycles.
  • Incomplete Solutions: Hardware manufacturers typically lack training capabilities, forcing customers to source operator development separately and delaying mission readiness.
  • Supply Chain Opacity: Multi-tier supplier relationships obscure component origins, preventing customers from validating NDAA compliance or Blue UAS Framework eligibility.

Forge & Flight Holdings addresses these challenges through strategic ownership of complementary capabilities: domestic electronics manufacturing ensures supply chain security, dedicated test infrastructure accelerates development cycles, integrated training delivery creates complete mission readiness, and centralized IP ownership protects innovations while enabling rapid technology deployment.

Portfolio Development Approach

Operating companies are formed, capitalized, and activated in phases based on customer demand, regulatory requirements, and capital availability. This disciplined approach enables controlled growth while maintaining quality standards.

  • Protection of Intellectual Property: Early formation of operating entities secures trade names and corporate identities before market presence, preventing competitor appropriation.
  • Controlled Regulatory Exposure: Activating operations only when customer contracts justify certification costs avoids premature compliance expenses.
  • Capital-Efficient Scaling: Phased activation aligns capital deployment with revenue generation, supporting sustainable growth without excessive working capital requirements.
  • Mission-Aligned Execution: Operating companies launch when specific customer programs or market opportunities justify dedicated focus and resources.

Quality & Compliance Philosophy

Forge & Flight Holdings establishes enterprise-level quality frameworks and compliance oversight that enable operating companies to pursue certifications appropriate to their specific customer requirements and contractual obligations.

Formal certifications, registrations, and approvals are maintained at the operating-company level as required by contract scope, regulatory environment, and customer specifications. This approach balances enterprise-wide standards with operational flexibility, allowing each operating company to pursue certifications that deliver customer value without imposing unnecessary compliance costs across the portfolio.

Our Vision

To build a resilient aerospace group capable of supporting advanced unmanned and aerospace systems from concept through flight—while developing the infrastructure, workforce, and operational maturity required for long-term success in an increasingly complex and strategically important industry.

As American aerospace and defense priorities shift toward domestic manufacturing, supply chain transparency, and technological sovereignty, Forge & Flight Holdings positions itself as a strategic partner capable of delivering integrated solutions that address these evolving requirements.
