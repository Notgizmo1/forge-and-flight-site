Electronics Manufacturing Division

NDAA-compliant avionics and electronics manufacturing for unmanned aircraft systems. 100% domestic component sourcing with complete supply chain traceability for defense and government procurement.

Core Capabilities

  • Custom avionics design and integration
  • PCB fabrication with domestic component sourcing
  • Power distribution and battery management systems
  • Communication systems and telemetry hardware
  • Complete component traceability documentation
  • NDAA Section 848 compliance verification

Supply Chain Security

  • 100% domestic electronic component sourcing
  • Verified supply chain documentation for every unit
  • Blue UAS Framework eligibility
  • No foreign dependencies on flight-critical systems
  • BOM and traceability records provided at delivery

Division Status

The Electronics Manufacturing Division currently operates as an internal division of Forge & Flight Holdings, Inc., supporting platform production for Forge and Flight Labs. Formal standalone operations and external customer services are planned for 2026 as capital permits.

For NDAA compliance documentation on platforms produced by Forge and Flight Labs, contact us directly.
